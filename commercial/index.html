Alexandria Water Heater handles food-service hot water, including high-input gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, correct sizing by peak-hour demand, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Alexandria Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Alexandria and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on tank, tankless, and boiler-fed systems. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water fast, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we set up a maintenance schedule so it doesn't happen again, and each response comes with full documentation for your facility files.

Ongoing Protection

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Alexandria Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve tests, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Call us to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
