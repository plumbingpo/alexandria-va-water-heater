How Falls Church Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Many early failures trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Drain and flush the tank once a year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and operate the relief valve annually so it doesn't seize. Keep the temperature near 120 degrees to reduce scald risk and slow scale buildup. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard so the heat exchanger stays clean.

The age of your heater matters just as much. With regular service, a standard tank usually runs 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts acting up, like discolored hot water, rumbling, or a damp spot around the base, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than an emergency swap after a leak.

Left alone, a small problem tends to grow. Consider a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank steel, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a flooded utility room.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

In Falls Church, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out heating elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale buildup, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. Our technicians work on all four every week and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
