How Manassas Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on descaling every year in hard-water areas.

The age of your heater matters just as much. With regular maintenance, a tank water heater typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like discolored hot water, rumbling, or a damp spot under the tank, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new heater with water spreading across the floor.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to high water pressure or a missing expansion tank, both modest fixes. The same drip left alone for a season can wear out the valve and stress the tank until the tank itself fails, and a small repair becomes a full replacement and a cleanup. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Manassas

In Manassas, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
