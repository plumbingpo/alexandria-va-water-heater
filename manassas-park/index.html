Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Manassas Park

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush every year in hard-water areas.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms such as discolored hot water, popping and rumbling, dampness under the tank, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one after the tank lets go.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to high water pressure or a missing expansion tank, both inexpensive fixes. The same drip left alone for a season can wear out the valve and stress the tank until a seam gives way, and a small repair becomes a full replacement and a cleanup. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and cold showers.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

Every type of water heater has its own weak points, and we work on all of them in Manassas Park. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, dirty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks lose elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
