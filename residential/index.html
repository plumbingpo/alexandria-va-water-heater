Hot Water at Home

The Water Heater Team Homeowners Rely On

A busy household runs through a surprising amount of hot water every day, so a failing water heater disrupts the whole routine, from morning showers to evening laundry. Left alone, a heavy layer of sediment or a weeping relief valve can turn a nuisance into a flooded utility room. When something goes wrong, you need a team that responds quickly, pins down the fault accurately, and fixes it right the first time. That's the standard Alexandria Water Heater holds itself to.

Whether you're dealing with a gas unit whose pilot keeps going out, an electric tank with a dead heating element, or a tankless unit flashing an error code, our experienced technicians have the know-how and parts to handle just about any household water heater repair.

  • Gas, electric, and heat pump units

No Surprises

Straight Answers and Upfront Prices

No homeowner likes a surprise on the invoice. So Alexandria Water Heater quotes every job upfront, after a full diagnostic that covers the heater's age, fuel type, and capacity, the burner assembly or heating elements, the anode rod and relief valve, and the venting and supply lines. You'll know the problem, what the fix involves, and exactly what it will cost before you say yes.

Most water heater failures give quiet warnings long before the tank quits. Keep an eye out for discolored or rusty hot water, a rumbling tank, hot water that smells like rotten eggs, or showers that go cold sooner than they used to. Behind those symptoms are usually a thick layer of sediment, a spent anode rod, or a worn dip tube. An early service call at the first sign usually costs far less than mopping up after a ruptured tank.

How old is your water heater? The date code on the label usually gives it away. Once a tank passes the 10-year mark, it's worth scheduling a replacement before the current one forces the issue. For reference, a 40 to 50 gallon gas tank typically runs $1,400–$2,800 installed, and an electric tank typically runs $1,100–$2,400, depending on venting, code updates, and access. Our team will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is running on borrowed time.

Annual Flushing

Tank Flushing and Tune-Ups That Pay Off

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep hot water flowing all year. Regular service prevents failures before they start, flushes out mineral sediment, and catches small faults while they're still inexpensive to fix. At Alexandria Water Heater, every maintenance visit is built around your actual equipment, covering a full tank flush, an anode rod inspection, a test of the temperature and pressure relief valve, a look at the burner and flue on gas models, element and thermostat testing on electric units, and a temperature check at the tap.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with annual service for most tanks and twice-yearly flushing where the water is hard. That's especially important where mineral content is high, where sediment coats the tank floor, makes the burner work harder, and wears out the tank. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians tailor the plan to your equipment's age, your household's hot water demands, and your local water.

Homeowners are often surprised how much a consistent maintenance schedule helps. A clean tank heats water faster and more quietly, a healthy anode rod keeps rust from attacking the steel, and a working relief valve protects the whole house. Most families find that annual service costs far less than replacing a water heater even one year early.

Sized for Your Household

A Water Heater Sized for Your Actual Household

Proper sizing is one of the most overlooked parts of any install. Before quoting a new heater, Alexandria Water Heater looks at the details that determine your household's peak demand: how many people and bathrooms you have, back-to-back showers, when the laundry and dishwasher run, and how cold the water entering the house gets. The result is a unit with a first-hour rating or GPM capacity to handle your busiest hour without wasting energy on excess capacity.

Households change over time. A new baby, a teenager, or an in-law suite can outgrow the tank that once kept up just fine. Tankless water heaters heat water on demand and don't run out as long as you stay within the rated flow, while hybrid heat pump tanks use far less electricity than a standard electric tank. Whether you're comparing tank and tankless, or gas and electric, we'll explain the real trade-offs in installed price, operating cost, venting and gas line needs, and installation work.

Frequent Failures

Hot Water Problems We Solve Every Week

Homes in Alexandria run into a familiar set of water heater failures, and each one calls for a different fix. Alexandria Water Heater handles them all: no hot water, water that never gets hot enough, pilots that won't stay lit, electric tanks with burned-out elements, noisy tanks full of sediment, rusty or smelly water, leaking relief valves, and tankless units flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A bad element only means lukewarm showers. A relief valve that discharges, on the other hand, is a safety device doing its job and telling you something is wrong, and water pooling under the tank can soak into flooring before anyone notices. Smell gas? Leave the house and call your gas utility before anything else. Our technicians carry the parts to replace elements, thermostats, thermocouples, gas valves, and relief valves, add a thermal expansion tank when pressure is the culprit, and make leak repairs at fittings and drain valves.
